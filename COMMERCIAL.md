# Commercial licensing

**Using Fotohaus is free for everyone**: at home, at work, in a business, in a school. You don't
need to ask.

What needs a separate written license is **selling or commercially redistributing Fotohaus
itself**, for example:

- selling Fotohaus or a modified version, or charging for downloads or access;
- bundling or embedding Fotohaus (or a substantial part of its code) in a paid product, paid
  app-store listing, paid distribution or hardware bundle;
- offering a paid service or hosted product built mainly on Fotohaus.

Licenses for these cases are granted case by case, usually for a royalty or a license fee. To ask
for one, open an issue titled "Commercial license request" or contact
[@aelnagish](https://github.com/aelnagish) on GitHub.

Donations through Buy Me a Coffee are gifts. They don't grant a resale or bundling license.

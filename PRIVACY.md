Fotohaus privacy policy
=======================

Fotohaus works on your computer. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it never
uploads your photos, faces, names, captions or library anywhere. Face recognition, search, places
and editing all run on your computer.

Fotohaus only goes online in these three cases:

1. Map tiles, when you open the Places map.
   The map pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). OpenStreetMap sees
   your IP address and which part of the map is shown. You can turn this off in
   Tools > Options > General > Map ("Load OpenStreetMap tiles").
   OpenStreetMap's privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

2. Place search, only when you type a place into the search box of the Places map.
   The text you typed is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org)
   to find it on the map. Nothing is sent unless you search.
   Same privacy policy as above.

3. Natural-language search model, only if you choose to download it.
   The optional model is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). After that it runs on
   your computer; your photos and searches are never sent.
   Hugging Face's privacy policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy

Sharing and email go through the apps you pick, such as your mail program, and follow their
policies.

Questions: https://github.com/aelnagish/Fotohaus/issues

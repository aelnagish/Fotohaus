Every photo you’ve taken, at home on your Mac.

Fotohaus sorts the folders you already have into a library you can search, edit and enjoy. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is moved.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

Version 1.0.0 · free · Apple silicon, macOS 14 or newer

Flip through a folder in a blink

Double-click any photo in Finder and it opens straight away over your desktop. Press ← and → to move through the rest of the folder. There’s nothing to import first.

  • Zoom with the scroll wheel, star or rotate as you go
  • A filmstrip of the whole folder along the bottom
  • Esc takes you back to what you were doing

Find anyone, and anything

Type a name, a place, a year or what’s in the picture. Fotohaus recognises faces, objects, scenes and even the words in a photo, all on your Mac.

  • Name a face once and Fotohaus suggests the same person across the years
  • Search for “receipt” or a street sign and find the picture
  • Combine filters: person:anna place:zermatt year:2019

Edit without fear

One-click fixes, fine tuning and more than 40 effects. Your original is always kept, so every change can be undone, even years later.

  • Auto enhance, straighten, crop, red-eye and retouch
  • Light, shadows, highlights and colour temperature
  • Copy a look and paste it onto a whole batch

On this day

Each day Fotohaus gathers the pictures you took on today’s date in earlier years, ready to play as a slideshow. Every photo also knows where it was taken, worked out offline from a built-in atlas.

  • “2 years ago”, “3 years ago”, right at the top of your library
  • A Places map with real town names
  • Search for a town and see every photo from there

Private by design

There’s no account and no cloud. Faces, search and editing all run on your Mac, and your photos stay in the folders you put them in.

Private collection

Move pictures to Private and they disappear from the library, search and Memories. Opening it takes Touch ID or your Mac password.

Archive

Tuck away screenshots and receipts you want to keep but not see every day. They stay on disk and come back with one click.

Recently Deleted

Anything you delete waits 30 days before it goes to the Trash, so a slip of the finger is never final.

Coming from Picasa?

Point Fotohaus at your old Picasa folders and your stars, captions, albums, face tags and edits are all there. They’re read straight from the .picasa.ini files Picasa left behind, and Fotohaus keeps writing them, so nothing is locked in.

[Picasa] name=2019 Zermatt [IMG_2041.jpg] star=yes caption=First light over the Matterhorn filters=enhance=1;sepia=1; faces=rect64(3f845bcb59418507),8e62398ebda8c1a5

Reads JPEG, HEIC, AVIF, PNG, TIFF, WebP, GIF, PSD, camera RAW (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF and more) and common video formats. A command line, fotohaus-cli, and an MCP server let you search and organise from the Terminal or an AI assistant.

Install

Fotohaus runs on Apple silicon Macs (M1 or newer) with macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

  1. Download the disk image

    Fotohaus-1.0.0-arm64.dmg (200 MB), or browse all releases.

  2. Drag Fotohaus into Applications

    Open the disk image and drag the app onto the Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    Fotohaus isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before the first launch. If it says it could not verify Fotohaus, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

    If it says the app is damaged, click Cancel and run this once in Terminal: xattr -cr /Applications/Fotohaus.app. Nothing is wrong with the app; that’s how macOS treats apps from the internet that aren’t notarized.

Questions

Does Fotohaus upload my photos anywhere?

No. Face recognition, search, place names and editing all run on your Mac. There’s no account, no cloud and no tracking.

Will it move or change my files?

No. Fotohaus works on your folders where they are. Edits are stored alongside the photo and your original is kept, so you can always go back.

Can I use it alongside Apple Photos?

Yes. Fotohaus doesn’t touch the Photos library. Point it at any folders you like, including exports from Photos or Google Takeout.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

Not at the moment. Fotohaus needs an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 14 or newer.

Is it really free?

Yes. Fotohaus is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. You can use, study, share and change it.

How do I uninstall it?

Drag Fotohaus from Applications to the Trash. Your library data lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Fotohaus; delete that folder too if you want it gone completely. Your photos are never touched.

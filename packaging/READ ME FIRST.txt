FOTOHAUS — a private photo organizer for Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or newer)
==============================================================================

INSTALL
  Drag Fotohaus into the Applications folder.

FIRST LAUNCH
  This build is not notarized by Apple, so macOS may refuse to open it the first time.
  Either:
    • Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway"
      next to the message about Fotohaus, or
    • run this once in Terminal:
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fotohaus.app

  If macOS says the app "is damaged", the Terminal command above fixes it.

WHAT IT DOES
  • Watches the folders you choose (it never moves or uploads your photos).
  • Reads and writes Picasa's .picasa.ini files: stars, captions, albums, face
    tags and non-destructive edits from Picasa carry over (and back).
  • Finds and recognizes faces, objects, scenes and text on your Mac.
  • Search: "beach", "person:anna", "year:2019", "text:invoice", "is:starred" …
  • Duplicates finder, Places map, slideshow, collage, export with watermark,
    Google Takeout and Apple Photos (read-only) import.

COMMAND LINE / CLAUDE
  /Applications/Fotohaus.app/Contents/MacOS/fotohaus-cli --help
  MCP server for Claude Desktop (claude_desktop_config.json):
    "fotohaus": { "command": "/Applications/Fotohaus.app/Contents/MacOS/fotohaus-cli", "args": ["mcp"] }

Your library database lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Fotohaus
Logs (if something goes wrong at startup): ~/Library/Logs/Fotohaus.log

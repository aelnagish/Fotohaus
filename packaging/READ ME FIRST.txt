FOTOHAUS — a private photo organizer for Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or newer)
==============================================================================

INSTALL
  Drag Fotohaus into the Applications folder.

FIRST LAUNCH
  This build is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. To allow it:
    1. Open Fotohaus once (macOS shows a warning; click Done).
    2. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway"
       next to the message about Fotohaus, then confirm.
  Or run this once in Terminal:
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fotohaus.app

  If macOS says the app "is damaged", the Terminal command above fixes it.

WHAT IT DOES
  • Watches the folders you choose (it never moves or uploads your photos).
  • Double-click a photo in Finder and flip through the whole folder with ← and →.
  • Finds and recognizes faces, objects, scenes and text on your Mac.
  • Memories ("on this day"), place names worked out offline, stacks for RAW+JPEG,
    bursts and Live Photos, Archive, a Touch ID-locked Private album, and
    Recently Deleted (30 days to change your mind).
  • Search: "beach", "person:anna", "place:zermatt", "year:2019", "is:starred" …
  • Editing without touching originals, collages, slideshows, movies with music,
    duplicates finder, Places map, backups, Google Takeout import.
  • Reads and writes .picasa.ini files: stars, captions, albums, face tags and
    edits from Picasa carry over.
